Universe

Taken from the design document

  • Seed tree (§1.1): every sector, region and value comes from hashing its parent's seed with its own address part or name.
  • Clustering (§2.2): a smooth density field decides which sectors hold a system, so systems gather into clusters with voids between. A sector keeps its system only if at most two of its eight neighbors also rolled one, so no system has more than two adjacent systems.
  • Sectors (§2.1): 21 × 21 regions, at most one system, its center in region 0, 0.
  • Planet placement (§4.1): coarse and fine fields along the orbital radius, cut by a threshold; most systems get 4 or 5 planets.
  • Biomes (§4.2): picked by orbit, hot worlds inward and cold worlds outward.
  • Deep space (§3.3): rock and ice asteroid fields, pirate outposts and wormholes; Open Space holds asteroid fields.
  • Wormholes (§3.4): space is split into blocks of 64 × 64 sectors, each with one spot region in its middle half, so two ends are always at least 32 sectors apart. Blocks fall into nine interleaved layers, each paired within its own groups by a Feistel cipher with the hinge (N − 1 − n) xor 1, so every block has exactly one partner and is its partner's partner. A pair is a wormhole when its spots are within its layer's reach, its roll is under the threshold and both spots are Deep Space. Every value comes from the block's own coordinates.

Chosen for this preview

  • Wormhole reach: two layers reach 300 to 500 sectors (groups of 4 × 4 blocks, 12 cipher rounds), three 300 to 1,000 (8 × 8), three 300 to 3,000 (16 × 16) and one 300 to 8,000 (32 × 32), so wormholes come in roughly 2 : 3 : 3 : 1 short to long. Each layer's threshold is set so about 1 block in 4 holds an end, and a 10 × 10-sector window shows one about once in 160 windows.
  • Drawing: the map opens at about 12 × 12 sectors on a wormhole end, close to the in-game starmap. Lines are colored by length. At most 40 wormholes are drawn at once, at full strength: the ones with the lowest rolls among those in view, so the same ones stay while panning and the map never turns into a tangle; the top corner says how many are shown. Hover a wormhole to see its length and ends, click it to keep it selected.
  • Rates for star types, asteroid fields and pirate outposts, and the size of clusters and voids, are placeholders; none are set in the doc yet.
  • Hash is a fast 32-bit one for the browser; the game uses 53-bit hashes with a server secret (Appendix C).
