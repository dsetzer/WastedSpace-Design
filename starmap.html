Universe

Taken from the design document

  • Seed tree (§1.1): every sector, region and value comes from hashing its parent's seed with its own address part or name.
  • Clustering (§2.2): a smooth density field decides which sectors hold a system, so systems gather into clusters with voids between.
  • Sectors (§2.1): 21 × 21 regions, at most one system, its center in region 0, 0.
  • Planet placement (§3.4): coarse and fine fields along the orbital radius, cut by a threshold; most systems get 4 or 5 planets.
  • Biomes (§3.2): picked by orbit, hot worlds inward and cold worlds outward.
  • Deep space (§3.6): rock and ice asteroid fields, pirate outposts and wormholes; Open Space holds asteroid fields.
  • Wormholes (§3.7): paired by a Feistel cipher, 300 to 500 sectors long, the same answer at both ends.

Chosen for this preview

  • Wormhole pairing uses the proposed version: areas of 25 × 25 sectors are paired, each with at most one wormhole end, and duplicate routes are dropped. About one wormhole per 400 × 4...
  • Each block has its own wormhole key, so blocks don't repeat the same pattern.
  • Rates for star types, asteroid fields and pirate outposts, and the size of clusters and voids, are placeholders; none are set in the doc yet.
  • Hash is a fast 32-bit one for the browser; the game uses 53-bit hashes with a server secret (Appendix C).
